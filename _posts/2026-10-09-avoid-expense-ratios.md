Yes, you can buy the underlying stocks directly, in approximately the same weights as an index, and periodically update your holdings to follow the index.

## But, there are hidden costs.

If you build your own index fund, you may incur costs:

- Tax consequences when you sell appreciated shares
- Commissions/spreads (depending on brokerage)
- Bid/ask spread
- corporate actions
- cash drag from being unable to buy exact fractional amounts
  
## It also adds complexity

- hundreds of individual positions to maintain
- difficulty perfectly reproducing the index
