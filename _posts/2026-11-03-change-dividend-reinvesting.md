title: How do I change my dividend settings?
date: 2026-11-03 10:00:00 -0000
category: "Vanguardd"
---

1. Log into Vanguard.com
2. Click on your Profile
3. Click on *Profile & account settings*
4. Click on *Accounts & trading*
5. Click on *Dividend & capital gains settings*
6. Scroll down to account.
7. Click on either
    *Edit holding-level dividend elections*
    *Edit holding-level capital gains elections*
8. Choose *Reinvest*, *Transfer to my settlement fund* or *Transfer to my bank account*
9. Choose the holdings from that account that you would like to apply your dividend election to
   Click *Continue*
